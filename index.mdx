---
title: "Introduction"
description: "UnPay is API-first financial and communication infrastructure — send payouts and run AI voice campaigns through one consistent REST API."
---

UnPay gives your product two production-grade capabilities behind a single,
consistent REST API:

- **Payouts** — disburse money to any bank account or UPI ID over IMPS, NEFT,
  RTGS and UPI, one at a time or in bulk.
- **AI Calling** — build AI voice agents and run outbound calling campaigns,
  then pull back transcripts, recordings and AI summaries.

Everything is server-to-server, authenticated with scoped API keys, and
tenant-isolated to your account.
