## What's the difference between `incoming` and `flow` agents?

- **`flow`** — outbound agents that run calling **campaigns** against a contact
  list.
- **`incoming`** — agents that **answer inbound calls** on a purchased number,
  with optional live transfer.

Set the `type` when you create the agent — it can't be inferred.

## How is usage billed?

Voice minutes are metered in **credits**. Check your remaining balance any time
with `GET /me`. Top up before launching a large campaign.

## What phone number format should contacts use?

E.164, e.g. `+919812345678`. Add contacts to a campaign with
`POST /campaigns/{campaignId}/contacts`. `customFields` are merged into the call
context for personalisation (e.g. an appointment time).

## Can I control when a campaign calls?

Yes. Set `scheduleEnabled: true` with `scheduleTimeStart` / `scheduleTimeEnd`
(HH:MM), `scheduleDays` and `scheduleTimezone`. Calls only happen inside that
window.

## Can failed calls be retried automatically?

Enable `retryEnabled` and tune `retryMaxAttempts` and `retryIntervalMinutes`.
The campaign re-dials contacts that didn't connect, within your retry policy.

## What data do I get back from a call?

Each call record includes `status`, `duration`, `recordingUrl`, `transcript`,
`aiSummary`, `sentiment` and `creditsUsed`. Fetch them with `GET /calls` or a
single `GET /calls/{id}`.

## What do the call statuses mean?

| Status | Meaning |
|---|---|
| `completed` | The call connected and finished. |
| `no_answer` | Nobody picked up. |
| `busy` | The line was busy. |
| `failed` | A technical error prevented the call. |

Use the corresponding [webhook](/guides/verify-webhooks) events
(`call.completed`, `call.no_answer`, `call.busy`, `call.failed`, …) to react in
real time.

## Why isn't my campaign dialing?

Check that the campaign has: an `agentId`, at least one contact, a caller-ID
number, and — if scheduling is on — that the current time falls inside the
call window. Also confirm your credit balance via `GET /me`.
