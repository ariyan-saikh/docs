## What is a wallet?

A wallet is the funding source for payouts. It exposes a balance, per-mode
availability (IMPS/NEFT/RTGS/UPI) and a `balanceSource`. List your wallets with
`GET /payouts/wallets`.

## What do the balance fields mean?

From `GET /payouts/balance`:

| Field | Meaning |
|---|---|
| `balance` | Total funds in the wallet. |
| `holdBalance` | Reserved for in-flight payouts. |
| `virtualBalance` | Virtual/credit component, where applicable. |
| `totalAvailable` | Computed as `balance + virtualBalance`. |

Remember a payout also debits fee + GST, and in-flight payouts show up as
`holdBalance` — leave headroom for both.

## What is Connected Banking?

Connected Banking wallets (`balanceSource: bank`) are linked directly to a
partner bank account (Yes Bank). For these, UnPay can read the **live** balance
from the bank rather than a ledger figure.

## Why is my ledger balance "not authoritative" for a bank wallet?

For `balanceSource: bank` wallets, money can move at the bank independently of
UnPay's ledger, so the ledger number can lag. Fetch the real-time figure with
`GET /payouts/wallets/{walletId}/bank-balance`.

## The live bank balance call returned an error

| Error | Meaning |
|---|---|
| `400 NOT_CONNECTED_BANKING` | The wallet isn't a Connected Banking wallet. |
| `400 NO_LINKED_ACCOUNT` | No bank account is linked to the wallet. |
| `404 WALLET_NOT_FOUND` | Unknown wallet id. |
| `502 BANK_UPSTREAM_ERROR` | The partner bank API was unavailable — retry with backoff. |

## How do I get a transaction statement?

Use `GET /payouts/statement` with optional `dateFrom`, `dateTo`, `type` and
`walletId` filters. Results are paginated (`page`, `pageSize` up to 200). See the
[Wallets & Connected Banking](/guides/banking) guide.

## How do I reconcile payouts against my statement?

Each payout carries a `referenceId` and (once processed) a
`providerTransactionId`. Match those against statement entries, and use
[webhooks](/guides/verify-webhooks) (`payout.completed` / `payout.reversed`) to
keep your ledger in sync in real time.
