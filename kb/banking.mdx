## What is an account?

An account is the funding source for payouts. It exposes a balance, per-mode
availability (IMPS/NEFT/RTGS/UPI) and a `balanceSource`. List your accounts with
`GET /payouts/accounts`.

## How do I check an account balance?

Use the single, provider-agnostic endpoint
`GET /payouts/accounts/{accountId}/balance` — there is **no default provider**,
you always pass the `accountId`. It returns the ledger balance for internal
accounts and the live bank `clearBalance` for Connected Banking accounts.

## What do the balance fields mean?

| Field | Meaning |
|---|---|
| `available` | Canonical spendable amount — size transfers against this. |
| `balance` | Total ledger funds (ledger accounts). |
| `holdBalance` | Reserved for in-flight payouts. |
| `totalAvailable` | Same as `available` (kept for compatibility). |
| `clearBalance` | Live bank balance (Connected Banking accounts). |
| `balanceSource` | `ledger` or `bank`. |

Remember a payout also debits fee + GST, and in-flight payouts show up as
`holdBalance` — leave headroom for both.

## What is Connected Banking?

Connected Banking accounts (`balanceSource: bank`) are linked directly to a
partner bank account (Yes Bank). For these, the balance endpoint reads the
**live** balance from the bank rather than a ledger figure.

## Why is my ledger balance "not authoritative" for a bank account?

For `balanceSource: bank` accounts, money can move at the bank independently of
UnPay's ledger, so the ledger number can lag. The balance endpoint
(`GET /payouts/accounts/{accountId}/balance`) handles this for you — it fetches
the real-time `clearBalance` automatically for these accounts.

## The balance call returned an error

| Error | Meaning |
|---|---|
| `400 ACCOUNT_ID_REQUIRED` | No account id supplied. |
| `404 ACCOUNT_NOT_FOUND` | Unknown account id (or not yours). |
| `409 PROVIDER_NOT_FOUND` / `PROVIDER_INACTIVE` | The account's provider is missing or disabled. |
| `400 NO_LINKED_ACCOUNT` / `UNSUPPORTED_RAIL` | Connected Banking isn't usable for this account. |
| `502 BANK_UPSTREAM_ERROR` | The partner bank API was unavailable — retry with backoff. |

## How do I get a transaction statement?

Use `GET /payouts/statement` with optional `dateFrom`, `dateTo`, `type` and
`walletId` (the account id) filters. Results are paginated (`page`, `pageSize`
up to 200). See the [Accounts & Connected Banking](/guides/banking) guide.

## How do I reconcile payouts against my statement?

Each payout carries a `referenceId` and (once processed) a
`providerTransactionId`. Match those against statement entries, and use
[webhooks](/guides/verify-webhooks) (`payout.completed` / `payout.reversed`) to
keep your ledger in sync in real time.
