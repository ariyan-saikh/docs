UnPay is **API-first financial and communication infrastructure**. It lets you
add two capabilities to your product without stitching together multiple
vendors:

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="Payouts" icon="money-bill-transfer">
    Programmatic disbursements to bank accounts and UPI IDs over IMPS, NEFT,
    RTGS and UPI, with wallets, saved beneficiaries, statements and bulk (up to
    500 per call).
  </Card>
  <Card title="AI Calling" icon="phone-volume">
    AI voice agents and outbound calling campaigns, with contacts, scheduling,
    retries, and call records that include transcripts, recordings and AI
    summaries.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

## Who it's for

- **Platforms & marketplaces** paying out sellers, gig workers or vendors.
- **Fintechs & lenders** disbursing loans and refunds at scale.
- **Sales & support teams** automating outbound calls, reminders and surveys.
- **Partners & integrators** embedding payouts or voice into their own product.

## How access works

Everything is exposed through one REST API at
`https://api.unpay.co/v1`, authenticated with
[scoped API keys](/api-reference/authentication). You decide exactly what each
integration can do by granting [scopes](/api-reference/scopes), and you can
harden a key further with IP allowlists and
[request signing](/api-reference/request-signing).

## Reliability principles

| Principle | What it means for you |
|---|---|
| Idempotent payouts | Retries are safe — see [Idempotency](/api-reference/idempotency). |
| Per-key rate limits | Predictable, cluster-wide budgets — see [Rate limits](/api-reference/rate-limits). |
| Signed webhooks | Verify authenticity and reject replays — see [Verifying webhooks](/guides/verify-webhooks). |
| Stable error codes | Program against `code`, not prose — see [Errors](/api-reference/errors). |
